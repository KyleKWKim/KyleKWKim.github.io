Summary

I received my Ph.D. in Electrical and Electronic Engineering from Yonsei University, where I worked on multimodal learning for cancer prognosis using medical imaging, genomics, and clinical data.

I am currently a research scientist at the Clinical Robotics and Embodied AI Research (CREAI) Center at Samsung Medical Center (SMC). My research is expanding toward surgical robotics, Physical AI, and world models, with a focus on developing intelligent systems that can perceive, reason about, and interact with complex clinical environments.

More broadly, my research interests span medical artificial intelligence, multimodal learning, computer vision, surgical robotics, and embodied intelligence.
